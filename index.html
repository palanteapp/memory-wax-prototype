Legal and safety

How sure are we?

The same album can have dozens of pressings, and they differ in sound and value. Each record shows how certain Memory Wax is about yours.

● ConfirmedWe read the etched matrix in the deadwax and it matches the runout listed for one pressing. A strong match, not a guarantee.
◐ LikelyThe cover or barcode points to one pressing, and it’s probably right, but nothing we saw rules out another. Check the deadwax to be sure.
○ Album onlyWe know the album, not which pressing. Scan the deadwax whenever you want to pin it down.

Tap a record, then scan its deadwax, to move it up. These labels describe how sure the match is. They are not a promise about condition or authenticity.

Use your camera?

Memory Wax reads the deadwax, the cover or the barcode through your camera. This is the same step your phone asks for the first time.

What it doesLooks at the record in front of you to read it.
What it doesn’t doThe camera picture stays on your phone and is never saved or uploaded. When a barcode is read, only the number is sent to a public music database to look up the release.

Allowing opens your phone’s own permission prompt.

Our promises

What you can count on, written down so you can hold us to it.

Free to useScanning, your collection, your shelves and your export are free. No limit on how many records you scan or keep. No ads. If we ever add paid extras, they will be extras: your collection and your export stay free.
Your collection is yoursIt lives on your phone. You can take all of it with you at any time: a backup file with your photos, or a spreadsheet. Both are open formats that other tools can read.
Kept safe, without an accountBefore every update the app keeps a copy of your collection so you can go back. On iPhone it also keeps a copy in the Memory Wax folder in the Files app, which iCloud Backup includes.
If we ever shut downWe will tell you inside the app at least 90 days before. We will ship a final update that keeps everything working on your phone, and it will keep your export working. Nothing you have saved is held hostage.
Private by defaultWhat you paid and where you found a record never leave your phone. We don’t sell data, and there is no tracking in the app.

Privacy, copyright & contact

Plain-language summaries.

What Memory Wax isA tool for knowing what you hold: it identifies pressings, shows credits and keeps your collection. It doesn’t sell records or handle payment or shipping, and it can’t promise that a match is right, that a record is genuine, or what condition it is in.
Your content stays yoursYour notes, photos, prices and places stay on your phone. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
Copyright and cover artAlbum art, music, liner notes and photos belong to their owners. Covers are shown from the Cover Art Archive to help you recognise a record. Music plays in Apple Music’s own player, and we never store or stream audio. If you hold the rights to something shown here and want it removed, contact us.
PrivacyNo account, no ads, no tracking. The camera picture stays on your phone. Prices, places and your location never leave it. You must be 13 or older.

These are summaries, not legal documents.
